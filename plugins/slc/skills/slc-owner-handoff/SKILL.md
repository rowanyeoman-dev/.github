# Handing a step to the owner

- Put it at the top of the message, as numbered steps, never as a closing note.
- Each step gives the exact place to click and the exact value to enter.
- Name the owner issue, say what is blocked until it is done, and say how you will check it afterwards.
- Ask once. Do not repeat the reminder in every message. Carry on with work that does not depend on it.
- Do not ask the owner to decide something the owner has already decided or designed. Read the documentation, give one recommendation in plain words, and proceed when the rules and the design already answer it.
